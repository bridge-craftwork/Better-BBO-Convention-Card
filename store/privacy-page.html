Better BBO Convention Card — privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Better BBO Convention Card works on your convention cards in Bridge Base Online's card manager. It keeps nothing, and nothing leaves your browser except to Bridge Base itself, unless you click Edit on Bridge Craftwork or Save to Bridge Classroom.

What it does

It adds buttons to Bridge Base Online's convention card pages: printing that fits, the ACBL Classic and New PDFs, open in a tab, export and import, and links that open a card in the Bridge Craftwork card editor or in Bridge Classroom.

What it reads

  • Your cards, from Bridge Base's own card pages: their titles, partners and contents, the way Bridge Base's card manager reads them.
  • Bridge Base's session for the card manager, which Bridge Base puts in the address of its card pages. The extension uses it only to ask Bridge Base for your cards and to save them back, as the card manager does. It is not your password, which the extension never sees.
  • A file you choose to import, in your browser.

It does not read anything else on the page or in your browser.

What it keeps

Only your last choices in the Print and Export PDF dialogs (which ACBL card, the second page, green or black, fillable or not), so the next PDF starts from them, how you sort the card list and whether you hide its stock cards, and whether you use Bridge Classroom at bridge-classroom.org or .com, so Save to Bridge Classroom opens the one you're signed in on. They stay in the extension's own storage on this device, and go nowhere. Exports and PDFs are made in your browser and saved where your browser saves downloads. A save date is kept inside each card you save in Bridge Base's editor, on Bridge Base, so that an import can tell which copy is newer.

When anything leaves your browser

  • To Bridge Base, when you export, import or open a card: requests to Bridge Base's own card pages, with its session, as its card manager makes. After an import saves a card, the extension tells Bridge Base the card changed, as Bridge Base's editor does after a save, so your partners see the new version.
  • Edit on Bridge Craftwork, when you click it, opens that one card in the card editor at bridge-craftwork.com. Save to Bridge Classroom opens it in Bridge Classroom (bridge-classroom.org, or .com if that's where you use it), which saves it to your account there if you are signed in; the Bridge Classroom privacy policy covers it from there. Either way the card travels in the part of the link after the #, which your browser does not send to any server. A card holds what you typed into it, which can include your partnership's names.

Nothing else. No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Access to webutil.bridgebase.com/v2/Bridge Base Online's convention card pages, the only pages it works on
Safari only: access to www.bridgebase.com/v3/BBO's lobby, which holds the card pages. Nothing runs there; Safari greys out the extension's toolbar button on any site it can't reach
Access to bridge-classroom.org and bridge-classroom.comNotes which of the two you visit Bridge Classroom at, so Save to Bridge Classroom opens that one. Nothing is read from those pages
StorageRemembers your Print and Export PDF choices, the card list's sort, and that Bridge Classroom address, on this device

It asks for no other permissions.
