## Name
Better BBO Convention Card

## Promotional text
Print your Bridge Base Online convention cards without losing a column, get
them as ACBL PDFs, and back them up or move them with one click.

## Description
Better BBO Convention Card adds the things Bridge Base Online's convention card
manager is missing, right inside it: Account, then Convention cards, in BBO's
web lobby in Safari.

On a card (Print works on every card; the rest on ACBL cards, BBO's usual kind):
• Print that fits. BBO's Print clips the right-hand column; this prints the whole card across a landscape page.
• ACBL PDFs. Download the card as the ACBL Classic or the ACBL New convention card, filled in and ready to print or email, with what the card says in green as BBO shows it (or in black).
• Open in a tab. See and edit the card at full size instead of in BBO's narrow panel.
• Export JSON. Save a copy of the card in a portable format.
• Edit on Bridge Craftwork, or Save to Bridge Classroom. Open the card in the bridge-craftwork.com card editor, or in Bridge Classroom, which saves it to your account there.

On your list of cards:
• Export all your ACBL cards, and the ACBL cards partners have shared with you, in one JSON file.
• Import cards from a JSON file, from the bridge-craftwork.com editor's "Export for BBO" file, or from a PDF: one this extension or the bridge-craftwork.com editor made, or any filled-in ACBL convention card or Bridgodex PDF. A card that differs from one already on BBO is never replaced without asking.

The extension only runs on BBO's convention card pages, and only does anything
when you click. It talks to BBO with the session BBO's page already has; it
never sees your password. A card goes to Bridge Craftwork or Bridge Classroom
only when you click that button.

To start, open the app and turn on the extension in Safari, then allow it on
webutil.bridgebase.com when Safari asks.

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts. Open source.

## App Review notes (macOS)
In Safari, after enabling the extension in Safari > Settings > Extensions, also
allow it on webutil.bridgebase.com, using the extension's button in the toolbar
while the convention cards are open (BBO shows them in a frame from that site).
Safari asks per website; until it is allowed there, nothing appears, with no
error.

This extension works inside Bridge Base Online's convention card manager, which
needs a BBO account. A free account works and takes a minute to make at
https://www.bridgebase.com (Sign up). We don't supply ours: BBO allows only one
session per account, so a shared login would sign reviewers out of one another.

1. Sign in at https://www.bridgebase.com/v3/ (the web lobby). On the right, open Account, then Convention cards. Above the list of cards a toolbar appears: "Export my cards as JSON" and "Import from JSON or PDF". Each card row has a ↗ button that opens it in a new tab.
2. Click any card, then View (one of BBO's stock cards is fine). The card page gets a toolbar at the top right: Print (fixed) fits the whole card across a landscape page; PDF: Classic and PDF: New download the card as the ACBL convention cards; Export JSON downloads it; Edit on Bridge Craftwork and Save to Bridge Classroom open it on those sites.
3. Back on the list, "Import from JSON or PDF" with the file from step 2 adds the card to the account. Importing a card that already exists with different settings asks whether to keep, replace or keep both.

The extension asks for no permissions beyond access to webutil.bridgebase.com.
Its requests go only to BBO's own card pages, with the session the page already
has.
